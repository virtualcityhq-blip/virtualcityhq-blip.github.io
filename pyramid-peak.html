The mapped trail ends below the summit; the dashed line shows an approximate way to the top. The route-finding through the upper mountain is yours. We draw only the line the data supports.

The drawn line

The line across Pyramid Peak.

The line on this plate is recorded in 280 points. It measures 2.8 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 1.6 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. It stops 776.6 metres short of the summit point, where the mapped data ends.
