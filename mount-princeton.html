The drawn line

The line across Mount Princeton.

The line on this plate is recorded in 261 points. It measures 6.0 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 3.7 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies east of the summit, and the mapped data stops 4.1 metres short of the summit point.
