The drawn line

The line across Mount Bierstadt.

The line on this plate is recorded in 273 points. It measures 3.6 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 2.4 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies west northwest of the summit, and the mapped data stops 20.3 metres short of the summit point.
