The drawn line

The line across Pikes Peak.

The line on this plate is recorded in 294 points. It measures 11.5 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 5.9 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies east of the summit, and the mapped data stops 14.7 metres short of the summit point.
