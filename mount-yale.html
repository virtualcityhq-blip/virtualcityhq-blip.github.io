THE DRAWN LINE

The line across Mount Yale.

The line on this plate is recorded in 196 points. It measures 4.2 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 2.3 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies south southwest of the summit, and the mapped data stops 27.0 metres short of the summit point.
