The drawn line

The line across Missouri Mountain.

The line on this plate is recorded in 259 points. It measures 5.1 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 3.5 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies north of the summit, and the mapped data stops 3.5 metres short of the summit point.
