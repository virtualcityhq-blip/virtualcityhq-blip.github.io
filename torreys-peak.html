The drawn line

The line across Torreys Peak.

The line on this plate is recorded in 161 points. It measures 3.6 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 2.4 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies east northeast of the summit, and the mapped data stops 11.5 metres short of the summit point.
