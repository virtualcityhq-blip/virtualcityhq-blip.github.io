How these are made

These prints are AI-assisted illustrations informed by published climbing sources. The rock artwork and route lines are interpreted and reviewed for recognizable features and route sequence. They are decorative art, not surveyed topos or climbing guides; distances and positions are not to scale.

A climber's memory, kept with a surveyor's discipline.
