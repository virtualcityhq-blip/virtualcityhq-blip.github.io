The drawn line

The line across Uncompahgre Peak.

The line on this plate is recorded in 236 points. It measures 3.7 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 2.3 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies east southeast of the summit, and the mapped data stops 5.4 metres short of the summit point.

Survey notes

Where Uncompahgre Peak sits.

The highest of the 12 San Juan Mountains summits drawn here, 69 feet above the next one in the volume. One other summit on this list, Wetterhorn Peak, stands within five miles. The printed frame holds 2.9 miles of ground east to west and 3.8 north to south, cut at a 40 metre contour interval.
