The drawn line

The line across Maroon Peak.

The line on this plate is recorded in 298 points. It measures 5.5 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 3.2 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies northeast of the summit, and the mapped data stops 3.6 metres short of the summit point.
