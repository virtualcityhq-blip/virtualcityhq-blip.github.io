Policy

Privacy.

On sendlines.com, we do not set advertising cookies or run advertising pixels, and we do not sell personal data. Amazon logbook links include source-level measurement tags; Amazon’s privacy practices apply after you follow those links.

This website.

sendlines.com is a static site. We do not create customer accounts, set advertising cookies, run tracking pixels, or collect payment information. Fonts are self-hosted. Product links go to Etsy or Amazon, where the marketplace’s privacy policy governs purchases. Cloudflare provides the website infrastructure and may process technical request data. We use aggregate traffic information where available; browser-based visit measurement may be limited by browser settings or blockers. Logbook links pass through a source-specific Sendlines page before opening Amazon; visits to those pages are an outbound-interest measure, not a record of purchases or buyer identities. The checklist stores selected peak identifiers on your device only. These selections are not sent to Sendlines; use Clear checks to remove them. Campaign source labels are limited to website, Pinterest, Facebook or partner and do not identify you. Amazon Attribution tags on logbook links let Amazon report aggregate shopping activity attributed to each source, including purchases. Amazon may use cookies or other technologies on its own services to measure this activity under the Amazon Privacy Notice. Sendlines receives aggregate Attribution reports, not buyer identities.

Affiliate links.

Our field-notes guide contains Amazon Associates links marked “paid link.” These go directly to Amazon and include our store identifier; we do not assign identifiers to individual visitors. Amazon may use cookies or other technologies to attribute qualifying purchases under its Privacy Notice. We receive program reports, not buyer names or payment details. These links are separate from the logbook Amazon Attribution links. Read our affiliate disclosure.

Our Pinterest integration.

When our Pinterest app is authorized, it accesses only our own business account: account information, boards, and pins, using the minimum scopes needed to publish our own product pins and avoid duplicates. It does not access other users' data, follower lists, messages, or payment information. It performs no automated engagement.

OAuth tokens are stored securely on our own hardware, never embedded in client code, never logged, and never shared. No Pinterest data is retained beyond transient processing during a posting run. Revoking the app's access in Pinterest settings invalidates the tokens.
