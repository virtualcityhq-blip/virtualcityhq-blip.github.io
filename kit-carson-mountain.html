The drawn line

The line across Kit Carson Mountain.

The line on this plate is recorded in 283 points. It measures 6.6 miles as drawn, across a straight-line span of 3.3 miles. That is the mapped line on the paper, not the route as walked. Its far end lies west of the summit, and the mapped data stops 3.1 metres short of the summit point.
